Surgical programs

H4L supports high-impact general surgery and plastic and reconstructive surgery to restore function, prevent emergencies, and renew hope.

The surgical list

Priority general surgery procedures

Each procedure is selected for its clinical benefit, safety profile, and socioeconomic impact. Volunteer surgeons donate their professional fees, while H4L covers the institutional costs of care.

01

Inguinal hernia repair

Repair of a symptomatic groin hernia with mesh. Planned surgery prevents incarceration and strangulation, which can become life-threatening emergencies.

Restores the ability to lift, farm, and work.

02

Hydrocelectomy

Removal of a fluid collection around the testicle. A large hydrocele can make walking painful, restrict work, and cause serious social stigma.

Relieves pain, restores mobility, and protects dignity.

03

Cholecystectomy

Planned removal of a symptomatic gallbladder. Elective surgery avoids the far higher risk and cost of acute cholecystitis or gallstone pancreatitis.

Stops recurrent pain and prevents emergency admission.

04

Thyroidectomy (selected)

Removal of a non-malignant obstructive goiter after careful assessment. The procedure relieves pressure on the airway and swallowing passages.

Relieves compression and protects breathing.

05

Excision of benign tumours

Removal of symptomatic benign masses, including selected breast lesions, with histopathology where needed to confirm the diagnosis.

Provides clarity, relieves pain, and restores confidence.

Plastic & reconstructive surgery

Healing faces. Restoring function. Changing lives.

Alongside our priority general surgery procedures, H4L supports access to plastic and reconstructive surgery for people living with congenital, traumatic, and acquired conditions.

Conditions considered include cleft lip and palate, keloids and scar revision, burn deformities and contractures, facial and limb deformities, post-trauma reconstruction, skin and soft-tissue defects, nerve and tendon injuries, and tumours or lumps requiring reconstruction.

Every case is assessed individually by the clinical team. Contact us to discuss a referral and confirm assessment and treatment options.
