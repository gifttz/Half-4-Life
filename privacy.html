Last updated: 5 November 2026

Half 4 Life (“H4L”, “we”, “us”) is a charity project based at Lanz Clinic in Kakyeka, Mbarara City, Uganda. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use this website, send an inquiry, ask about a donation, or contact us by email or WhatsApp.

In short: this website does not store your form entries. The inquiry form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp so you can send it yourself. We use what you share only to respond, coordinate care or volunteering, and keep the records we need.

Who we are

Half 4 Life is a surgical charity project working with Lanz Clinic in Western Uganda. For privacy questions, the first point of contact is Dr. Buluke Brian, Founder and Public Relations Officer.

Information you give us

Depending on how you contact us, we may receive:

  • your name, email address, phone number, and the subject of your inquiry;
  • the message you choose to send us;
  • information about a donation, including the amount, date, bank reference, and receipt you share for acknowledgement;
  • information you provide when discussing volunteering, a partnership, or a patient referral;
  • any other information you choose to include in an email, phone call, or WhatsApp message.

Please do not send detailed medical records, patient photographs, or other sensitive information through the website form or ordinary WhatsApp unless we have agreed a secure way to receive it.

How the inquiry form works

The inquiry form on this website does not submit to a server and does not store your entries on the site. When you press “Send via WhatsApp”, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message prepared from the fields you filled in. You decide whether to send it.

WhatsApp is operated by Meta and has its own privacy terms. Messages sent through WhatsApp are processed by that service as well as by H4L. If you prefer not to use WhatsApp, you can email or call us using the contact details on the contact page.

How we use information

  • to respond to your inquiry and route it to the right person;
  • to discuss a patient referral, volunteer placement, or partnership;
  • to acknowledge donations and maintain financial records;
  • to keep records needed for clinical, financial, or legal purposes;
  • to protect patients, staff, volunteers, and the public; and
  • to meet our obligations under applicable law.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use your details for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only where it is needed. This may include members of the H4L team, clinical staff at Lanz Clinic involved in a referral, and service providers that support our work, such as WhatsApp/Meta, email providers, and our bank. We may also share information with professional advisers, auditors, or authorities where required by law or where necessary to protect someone’s safety.

Some service providers may process information outside Uganda. Their own privacy terms apply to that processing.

Our basis for handling information

We handle personal information with consent where consent is required, and for legitimate purposes such as responding to you, arranging care or volunteering, administering donations, and meeting legal or clinical obligations. We aim to follow Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and good data-protection practice.

How long we keep it

We keep information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was given, or for as long as the law or clinical and financial record-keeping requires. Inquiry records, donation records, and referral records may need to be kept for different periods. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it securely.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to a particular use. Email doctorbryan96@gmail.com with enough detail for us to find your records.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We may also need to keep some records where the law requires it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may have the right to complain to the relevant Ugandan data-protection authority.

Children and patient privacy

This website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect children’s information through the inquiry form. If a child needs to contact us, a parent or guardian should do so on their behalf.

Patient stories on this site are illustrative composites unless stated otherwise. Names and clinical details are changed. We publish identifiable patient stories or photographs only with appropriate consent, and consent can be withdrawn. If you are a patient or relative and want content about you corrected or removed, please contact us.

Cookies, fonts, and analytics

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and technical request data; Google’s privacy terms apply. Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, and email services take you to third-party services with their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our work develops. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
