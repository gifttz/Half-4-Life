Common questions

Who is eligible for H4L support?

Priority is given to uninsured individuals living below the national poverty line, single-parent heads of household, elderly dependents, and subsistence agricultural workers. A clinical social worker conducts a standardised socioeconomic screening before a patient is enrolled.

How are patients referred?

Patients reach us through village health teams (VHTs), regional clinic referrals, and community outreach. A referral is followed by clinical assessment, laboratory workup, and financial clearance before surgery is scheduled.

How do I know my donation reaches patients?

Clinical decisions and financial management are kept separate. Financial administration is structured for independent review, and every enrolled patient is tracked through 7-, 14-, and 30-day follow-up reviews. The unit-cost model shows exactly what each $175 covers.

Can surgeons and theatre staff volunteer?

Yes. We welcome general surgeons, anaesthetists, nurses, and theatre staff who can support scheduled operating lists. Send a message with your specialty, registration details, and availability.

How do I make a donation?

We recommend bank transfer directly to Lanz Clinic at Absa Bank Uganda (account number 6008658879). WorldRemit and Western Union are alternatives that send to Buluke Brian personally. See the How to donate section for details. For a receipt, email macieurshers@gmail.com.

Can I donate online?

This website does not process payments. You can use WorldRemit with the mobile-money details listed on our donation page, or donate by bank transfer or Western Union.

How do I raise a concern?

Read our safeguarding statement and contact Dr. Buluke Brian in confidence. If the concern involves the founder, use the alternate contact listed on that page. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
