These terms explain how this website works and how we handle referrals, donations, and content. By using the site, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please stop using the site.

Medical emergency? Do not use this website or its contact form for an emergency. Go to the nearest hospital or call local emergency services. The site cannot arrange emergency care.

Using this website

This website is operated by Half 4 Life, a charity project based at Lanz Clinic, Kakyeka, Mbarara City, Uganda. The site provides general information about our work and ways to contact us. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Medical advice and emergencies

Nothing on this website creates a doctor–patient relationship. Information about conditions, procedures, costs, and recovery is general and may not apply to a particular patient. A qualified clinician must assess every patient before any procedure.

If you or someone else has a medical emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call local emergency services immediately. Do not wait for a reply through the website, email, or WhatsApp.

Referrals and eligibility

Sending an inquiry or referral does not enrol a patient or guarantee surgery. Each referral is reviewed through clinical assessment, financial screening, and the H4L pathway. H4L may redirect a patient to another service or decline support where a case falls outside the programme or available capacity.

Please do not send detailed clinical records or patient photographs through the website form. Contact the team first so we can agree a safe way to share information.

Donations and financial gifts

Donations are voluntary. They are not payment for a specific service and do not guarantee a particular patient, procedure, or clinical outcome.

How to give

We recommend bank transfer directly to Lanz Clinic. The donate page also lists WorldRemit mobile-money transfers and Western Union as alternatives that send to a named individual, not directly to the clinic’s bank account. This website does not process payments directly. Before sending a gift, verify the recipient and details with the team if anything is unclear.

Receipts and acknowledgement

For bank transfers, please use “H4L donation” as your reference. Send your receipt to macieurshers@gmail.com so the team can acknowledge your gift. A transfer receipt is not the same as a tax receipt; ask the team if you need a particular document.

Monthly pledges

A monthly partner pledge is arranged with the team and fulfilled by bank transfer. It is not an automatic online subscription and can be changed by contacting us.

Refunds and errors

Donations are generally non-refundable once they have been allocated to care. If you believe a transfer was made in error, contact the Finance Director, Mrs Nankunda Mercy, immediately at macieurshers@gmail.com or +256 751 609 132. We will review the matter case by case, subject to our bank’s rules and applicable law.

Restricted or major gifts

Please speak to us before making a restricted gift or a major commitment so that we can confirm whether the purpose is feasible and how it will be reported.

The text, photographs, logo, and design on this site belong to Half 4 Life, Lanz Clinic, or the people who gave permission for their use. You may link to the site and share pages for non-commercial awareness-raising. Please do not republish substantial content or photographs without written permission.

Patient stories are illustrative composites unless stated otherwise. Names and details are changed for privacy. Photographs may show clinical team members or care settings and may not depict the people described in a story.

Privacy and safeguarding

Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information. Our Safeguarding Statement explains how we protect patients, children, and vulnerable adults, and how to raise a concern.

Accessibility

We want this site to be usable by as many people as possible. If you have difficulty reading or using any part of it, contact us and we will try to provide the information in another format.
