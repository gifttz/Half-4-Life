About Half 4 Life

A charity project at Lanz Clinic, working to make safe general and reconstructive surgery accessible to low-income families in Western Uganda.

Home/About
Who we are

Surgery should not depend on a family’s savings.

Half 4 Life (H4L) was established in 2026 to bridge the wide systemic healthcare deficit in Western Uganda. From Mbarara, we serve poor patients with surgical needs from marginalised villages in Isingiro, Rwampara, Lyantonde, Kiruhura, Ibanda, Sheema, Ntungamo, Buhweju, and Kazo.

Our implementing partner is Lanz Clinic, a private specialised surgical centre in Kakyeka, Mbarara City. Started in 2020, Lanz provides affordable outpatient, inpatient, and theatre services. Its name comes from the Lanz incision used for appendectomy — a reminder that good surgery begins with a precise, careful approach.

H4L creates a streamlined pathway between volunteer surgical teams, accredited regional hospitals, and philanthropic partners. Our programs support priority general surgery as well as plastic and reconstructive surgery for congenital, traumatic, and acquired conditions.

Vision & mission

The change we are working toward.

Our vision

A Uganda where no patient is denied life-changing surgery because of poverty.

No patient should be denied life-changing, essential elective surgery due to financial hardship or poverty. We believe every life has value, and every family deserves the restoration that surgery can bring.

Our mission

Mobilise people and institutions around safe surgical care.

To mobilise volunteer surgical teams, healthcare facilities, institutional donors, and community leaders to provide equitable, transparent, and high-quality access to safe general and reconstructive surgery for low-income Ugandan populations.

Clinical pathway

From first screening to the 30-day review.

Every patient moves through a documented six-stage protocol. No stage is skipped, and every follow-up is recorded.

01

Community identification & screening

Outreach through village health teams (VHTs) and regional clinic referrals.

02

Clinical assessment & workup

Pre-operative evaluation by volunteer surgeons, laboratory diagnostics, and imaging.

03

Financial clearance & enrolment

Verification of poverty status and authorisation of the H4L non-clinical funding subsidy.

04

Surgical intervention

Elective procedure in an accredited partner theatre under WHO safety protocols.

05

Inpatient recovery & discharge

Post-operative nursing care, pain management, and wound monitoring before safe discharge.

06

Post-discharge follow-up

Mandatory clinical reviews at 7, 14, and 30 days to verify recovery and screen for infection.

Accountability

A credible model for patient care.

We believe trust is built through clear process, ethical decision-making, and consistent follow-up. That is why every case is reviewed before surgery, during recovery, and after discharge.

01 · Case review

Patients are screened before surgery.

Every referral is assessed for clinical priority and socioeconomic need so support reaches the patients who are most likely to benefit.

02 · Separated oversight

Clinical and financial decisions are distinct.

Doctors decide what care a patient needs; financial administration tracks costs and disbursement, creating clear separation and accountability.

03 · Follow-up review

Recovery is monitored after discharge.

Patients are reviewed at 7, 14, and 30 days to check wound healing, complications, and whether the treatment pathway remains safe and effective.

Governance

Clinical independence. Financial transparency.

H4L separates clinical decision-making from financial management. That dual structure protects clinical independence and gives institutional donors clear visibility over how funds are used.

  • The Clinical Steering Committee is led by Dr. Buluke Brian and includes senior surgical specialists and nurse managers.
  • The committee oversees surgeon credentialing, surgical protocol enforcement, morbidity and mortality reviews, and infection control.
  • Financial administration and donor disbursements are subject to independent financial review by qualified professionals.
